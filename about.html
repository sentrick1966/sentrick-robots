One platform. Many verticals.

Sentrick Robots is built on the Sentrick Behavior Intelligence Platform — the same AI foundation that protects people, property, and now robots.

Sentrick Robots is a product of Anima Technology Sensor & Tracking Solutions, LLC (Houston, Texas). It is one vertical in a single, shared platform — the Behavior Intelligence Platform (BSIP™) — that turns raw sensor data into calibrated, explainable risk classification.

The idea

Most robot systems tell you where the robot is. They don't tell you whether something on your site needs attention. Sentrick was built to close that gap: instead of only streaming video, it continuously learns what "normal" looks like and flags the moment something changes — so you act on real events, not noise.

The platform family

The same core — AI Edge, Cloud Brain, BSIP, Color Status, apps, billing, and open APIs — powers a growing family of products:

  • Sentrick — personal safety & wearable intelligence (live on iOS and Google Play)
  • Sentrick Shield — AI vision & smart property security
  • Sentrick Cargo — asset & cargo behavior intelligence
  • Sentrick Drones & Sentrick Robots — autonomous patrol and monitoring
  • Sentrick Fleet & Sentrick Industrial — on the roadmap

One reusable intelligence foundation, many markets — one cloud, one login, one bill.

Where we're headed

We are bringing autonomous patrol into the same behavioral-AI platform as our cameras and trackers, and moving manufacturing in-house over time. Government and public-safety programs are on our roadmap.
