How it works

Scan → activate → go live

Every Robots robot ships with a unique SRB- barcode that links the robot to your account, our cloud, and your plan.

Scan the barcode

The QR on the robot opens your Sentrick activation page.

Sign in

Log in or create your Sentrick account in seconds.

Bind to the cloud

The robot is registered to your account on the Sentrick cloud automatically.

Start your plan

Confirm the monthly subscription — secure checkout.

Go live

Deployments, video, and alerts flow to your dashboard.
