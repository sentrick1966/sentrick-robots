Catalog framework

Devices that work as one system

The catalog is organized by the role each product plays. Every listed product must also show how it connects to Sentrick, so customers can distinguish Sentrick-native hardware from qualified compatible products and accessories.

Native

Sentrick Native

Connects directly to Sentrick and supports the published capabilities, events and commands for its model.

Compatible

Sentrick Compatible

Qualified for Sentrick through a documented open protocol or approved integration adapter.

Accessory

Accessory

A sensor, payload or peripheral attached to a core device or gateway.

Native

Core Devices

AMRs, AGVs, patrol robots and charging docks.

Accessory

Sensors & Accessories

LiDAR, UWB, safety scanners, cameras and conveyor/door interfaces.

Compatible

Compatible Devices

Qualified VDA5050, ROS/ROS2 and MQTT robotic systems.

Qualification rule: no product card is published until its model, integration path, supported capabilities, compliance status, availability and media rights are verified. This feature branch intentionally introduces the catalog structure without inventing products or specifications.
