Sentrick Robots is one vertical of the Sentrick Behavior Intelligence Platform by Anima Technology.

Anima Technology is building one behavioral-AI platform that spans personal safety, property security, cargo, robots, and more — reusing a single cloud, billing system, and AI foundation across every product.

The thesis

Instead of a separate app and backend per product, Sentrick runs every vertical on one shared platform. That keeps engineering leverage high and lets each new market — including autonomous patrol and monitoring — launch on infrastructure that already exists.
