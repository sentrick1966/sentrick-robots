How it works

Scan → activate → deploy

Every Sentrick Robot ships with a unique SRB- barcode. That barcode is the key that links the unit to your account, our cloud, and your monthly plan.

Scan the barcode

The QR on the robot opens your Sentrick activation page.

Sign in

Log in or create your Sentrick account in seconds.

Bind to the cloud

The robot is registered to your account on the Sentrick cloud automatically.

Start your plan

Confirm the monthly subscription — secure checkout, cancel anytime.

Deploy & monitor

Set the patrol route and go live. Patrols, video, and alerts flow to your dashboard.
