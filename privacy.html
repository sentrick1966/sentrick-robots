Privacy Policy

Sentrick Robots™ — a product line of Anima Technology: Sensor & Tracking Solutions, LLC · Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Anima Technology: Sensor & Tracking Solutions, LLC ("Anima," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with Sentrick Robots™ hardware, the Sentrick cloud platform, and our websites and apps (together, the "Services").

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email, organization, and login credentials you provide when you create a Sentrick account.
  • Device and telemetry data — data your Sentrick device sends to our cloud, such as status, location, sensor readings, alerts, and diagnostic logs.
  • Payment information — subscription and order payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage data — how you interact with our dashboard and sites, including device identifiers, log data, and cookies used to keep you signed in and improve the Services.

How we use information

  • To operate your devices, deliver alerts, and provide the monitoring dashboard.
  • To process orders, subscriptions, activation, and support requests.
  • To secure the platform, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.
  • To improve reliability and develop new features.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers who help us run the Services (for example, cloud hosting, Stripe for payments, and email delivery), with fulfillment partners strictly to ship your order, and where required by law. Fulfillment partners receive only the shipping details needed to deliver your order.

Data retention & security

We retain data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services and meet legal requirements. We use industry-standard safeguards to protect data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can access, correct, export, or delete your account data by contacting us. You may cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard. Disabling cookies may limit some features.

Operator responsibilities

Sentrick Robots™ are offered for commercial deployments. Site owners are responsible for safe operating conditions and for complying with privacy, safety, and surveillance laws in their jurisdiction when capturing imagery or data.

Children

The Services are intended for business use and are not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
