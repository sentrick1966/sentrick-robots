Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Sentrick Robots™ is operated by Anima Technology Sensor & Tracking Solutions, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide (name, company, email, and pilot details) and operational data from robots you enroll on your account — patrol and flight logs, location and route data, video and imagery you capture, and AI detection events — used to deliver autonomous patrol.

How we use it

  • To provide autonomous patrol, live view, alerts, and dashboards.
  • To improve our detection models and service quality.
  • To communicate with you about your account, pilot, or support requests.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share information only with service providers who help us operate the platform (including our payment processor, Stripe), or where required by law.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work continuously to safeguard your information.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time. See our account & data deletion page.
